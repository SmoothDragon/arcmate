# ArcMate quick reference

*Print one page for the table. Full rules: [RULEBOOK.md](RULEBOOK.md). Terms: [GLOSSARY.md](GLOSSARY.md).*

---

## Start

1. Shuffle deck → deal **1** (learn) or **2** (usual) rule cards **face up in order**.
2. Read each card; apply in order (later cards stack on earlier).
3. Set up chess (normal or Fischer Random — see rulebook §8).
4. Card says **draw another card** → draw one more (unless you use “first `+` only” — say so now).

---

## Your turn

- Usually **one action**: one **move** OR one **capture**.
- Some cards: **two** moves, **two** captures, or **one move + one capture** = **one turn** → say **“done”** when finished.
- **No check.** You **win** if opponent has **no king** at end of your turn; you **lose** if you have no king.
- **Move Twice:** White **not** on move 1. **Castling** = **king** move.
- Use **move / capture / act** — not “take” or “attack.”

---

## Master rules (3 lines)

1. Rules describe how pieces work **right now** (all dealt cards, in order).
2. **Orthodox piece** = type it started as.
3. If stuck: deal order wins → agree → or remove last card.

---

## `+` corner

**Draw one more rule card** when the card says so (after dealing that card).

---

## Bughouse (4P)

Same dealt cards on both boards unless agreed. Partner passes captures as usual. Demo clocks often **5+5**.

---

## Words

| Say | Don’t say |
|-----|-----------|
| capture | take, attack |
| move | take a move |
| act | — |
| may **only** act like Y | (replacement) |
| may **also** act like Y | (addition) |

## Demo pitch (15 sec)

> Chess plus rule cards you deal in order. One or two cards per game, ten minutes with a clock. I’ll read the cards — ask anytime.
